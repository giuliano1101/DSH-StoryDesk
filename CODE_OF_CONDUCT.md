# Community Code of Conduct / 社区行为准则

We want a useful, respectful place for authors, developers and translators. Welcome different skill levels, languages and creative approaches. Discuss the work with evidence and specific suggestions; respect an author's right to reject an aesthetic suggestion.

Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, dox, spam or publish someone else's private manuscripts, credentials or personal information. Attribution and permission matter, including for AI-assisted work.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close threads, or restrict participation to protect the community. Decisions should be proportionate and explain the relevant conduct without exposing private reports. The policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions and other official project spaces.

若需举报涉及隐私或骚扰的行为，请使用 GitHub 的私密举报渠道；涉及代码安全的问题按 [安全说明](SECURITY.md) 提交。不要在公开 issue 中转贴受害者隐私。普通协作争议可在原讨论中请求维护者介入。See [GitHub reporting guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

中文、English、日本語 contributors are welcome. Respectful disagreement is part of making the project better.
